Community guidelines

Friendly is a place to make friends and practise English. These rules keep it safe, kind and welcoming for everyone.

Last updated 3 November 2026

The short version: be yourself, be kind, keep it friendly, and never ask anyone for money. If someone makes you uncomfortable, block or report them. You don't need a reason.

1Friendship, not dating

Friendly is for making friends. Don't use it to flirt, ask people out on dates, or look for a partner. Never send sexual messages, photos or requests.

2Adults only

You must be 18 or older to use Friendly. Don't lie about your age.

3Be yourself

  • Use your real first name and your real age.
  • Only post photos of yourself that you have the right to share. No nudity or sexual photos.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else, and only have one account.

4Be kind and patient

Many people here are practising English. Everyone makes mistakes, and that's how we learn.

  • Never make fun of someone's accent, grammar or spelling.
  • Only correct someone's English if they ask you to, and do it kindly.
  • If someone doesn't reply or declines your hello, respect it and move on.

5Respect everyone

No hate speech, bullying, harassment or threats. Don't treat anyone badly because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age or visa status.

6No scams, selling or spam

  • Never ask anyone for money, gifts, bank details or passwords. If someone asks you, report them.
  • Don't promote a business, product, investment, crypto scheme, job offer or visa service.
  • Don't send the same message to lots of people.

7Protect privacy

  • Don't share other people's messages, photos or personal details without their permission.
  • Keep your own private details safe. Never share your passport, visa or bank details, and wait until you trust someone before sharing your address.

8Nothing illegal or dangerous

Don't use Friendly to buy, sell or encourage anything illegal, such as drugs or weapons, and don't post anything violent or encourage people to hurt themselves or others.

9Meeting up safely

Meeting a new friend in person can be great. Stay safe when you do:

  • Meet somewhere public and busy, like a café, library or shopping centre.
  • Tell a friend where you're going and who you're meeting.
  • Get yourself there and back. Don't rely on someone you've just met for a lift.
  • If you feel uncomfortable, it's OK to leave.

10If something goes wrong

  • Block: tap the ••• button on someone's profile or chat, or "Block or report" on Discover. You won't see each other any more, and they won't be told.
  • Report: use the same menu and choose Report. Only the Friendly team sees reports, and we don't tell anyone who reported them.
  • Email us at hello.friendly.au@gmail.com about anything serious.
  • If you're in danger, call 000 (police, fire or ambulance in Australia).

For online abuse, you can report it to the eSafety Commissioner at esafety.gov.au/report. If you're struggling and need to talk to someone, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

11What happens if someone breaks these rules

We review every report. Depending on what happened, we may remove content, warn the person, or remove their account. If someone may be in danger, we may contact the police.
