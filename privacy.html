Privacy policy

What information Friendly collects, why we need it, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version: we only collect what the app needs to work. We don't show ads, we don't use tracking or analytics tools, and we never sell your information. Friendly is run by the Friendly team, and you can reach us at hello.friendly.au@gmail.com.

1Who we are

Friendly is a friendship and English-practice app for adults aged 18 and over, mainly for international students, migrants and newcomers in Australia. It is run by the Friendly team. Friendly is currently a test version used by a small group of people.

If you have a question about your privacy, email hello.friendly.au@gmail.com.

2What we collect

Your account. Your email address and password. Your password is stored by our login provider, Supabase, in a scrambled form that we can't read.

Your profile. What you choose to add: your first name, age, city, English level, languages you speak and are learning, bio, where you're from, how long you've been in Australia, work, study, what you're looking for, how you like to connect, personality traits, star sign, birthday (day and month only, never the year), the choices for your cartoon avatar, interests, prompt answers, and up to 6 photos with captions. Many of these are optional.

What you do in the app. The hellos you send and receive (including which photo or answer you said hi to, and any message you add), whether a hello was accepted, your chat messages, stickers, reactions and game answers, and when messages were read, the people you block, and any reports you send.

Photos and voice messages you send in chats. These are stored privately with Supabase and can only be opened by you and the friend you sent them to. If you unsend one, it's deleted.

Video calls. Calls go directly between your phone and your friend's phone where possible, and we don't record them. To connect a call, the two phones exchange technical connection details through our Supabase service, and a Google connection-helper server sees your IP address. We keep a short record in the chat, such as "Video call · 3 min" or "Missed video call".

Active status. While you have Friendly open, the app records when you were last active, so your friends can see things like "Active now" or "Active 2h ago". You can turn this off in Me → Show when I'm active. If you do, we stop recording it and you won't see other people's either.

Edited and unsent messages. You can edit or unsend a message for 15 minutes after sending it. When you do, we privately keep the original wording so the Friendly team can check it if someone reports a problem. Other members can't see it.

On your device. Your browser saves your login session and a list of profiles you skipped, so you stay logged in and don't see the same people again. This stays on your device.

What we don't collect. We don't collect your exact location (only the city you type), your contacts, or any payment details.

3How we use it

  • To run the app: logging you in, showing your profile to other members, suggesting people with shared interests, and delivering hellos and messages.
  • To keep people safe: reviewing reports and acting when someone breaks our Community guidelines.
  • To email you about your account, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.
  • To find and fix problems with the app.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

4Who can see what

  • Other members who are logged in can see your profile and photos. People you've blocked, and people who've blocked you, can't see you. Your email address is never shown to other members.
  • Your active status can only be seen by your friends (people you've connected with), and only if you both have it switched on.
  • Your birthday, if you add it, is stored with your profile like your other details. The app only uses it to celebrate with you on the day and to show your friends a 🎂.
  • Hellos can only be seen by you and the person you said hi to.
  • Messages, photos, voice messages and video calls in a chat can only be seen by you and the friend you're chatting with.
  • Reports can only be seen by the Friendly team. We don't tell anyone who reported them.
  • The Friendly team can access the app's database to run and protect Friendly. We only look at messages when we need to, for example to investigate a report or fix a problem.

5Services we use

We use a few outside services to run Friendly. They receive some information so the app can work, and each has its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase stores accounts, profiles, photos, hellos and messages, and sends our account emails. Our database is hosted in Sydney, Australia.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files. Like most websites, it may record technical details such as your IP address and browser type when you open Friendly.
  • Google Fonts provides the app's fonts. Google receives your IP address when the fonts load.
  • MyMemory (run by Translated) translates messages when you tap Translate. Only the message you choose to translate is sent to them, along with our contact email. Nothing is sent unless you tap Translate.
  • Google STUN servers help two phones find each other to start a video call. They see your IP address, but not the call itself.
  • jsDelivr delivers a piece of code the app uses to talk to Supabase. It receives your IP address when the code loads.

6How long we keep it

We keep your information while your profile exists.

When you tap Delete my profile in the Me tab, we immediately delete your profile, photos, hellos, friendships, blocks and chats. This includes your chats with other people, so the messages, photos and voice messages disappear for them too, along with the original wording of any messages you edited or unsent.

Two things are kept after that. Your login (your email address) stays so you can come back later. Email us from that address if you want it removed too. Reports are kept for safety records. The link to the deleted profile is removed, but the name and details written in the report stay.

7Your choices

  • Change your profile at any time in Me → Edit profile.
  • Delete your profile at any time in Me → Delete my profile.
  • Email hello.friendly.au@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your information, to correct something, or to delete your login completely. Please email us from the address you signed up with, so we know it's you. We aim to reply within 30 days.

8Adults only

Friendly is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.

9Keeping your information safe

The app connects using encryption (HTTPS). Access rules in our database mean members can only see what this policy says they can. Photos are shared through private links that expire after 6 hours.

No system is perfectly secure. If you notice a problem, please tell us straight away.

10Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll also tell you in the app.
